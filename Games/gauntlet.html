01 — OVERVIEW

Game Description

GAUNTLET is a game about boss fights and everything that surrounds them. Each and every one of the 11 previewed boss fights is hand-crafted, with completely different gameplay mechanics that turn each fight into a unique experience. The fights range from a rhythm game to a full-on gambling casino, with each one trying to push the player in a different way. The bosses in GAUNTLET are all inspired by my friends, each with their own stories, personalities, and fighting styles. That is why I decided to make every fight special and tailored to the boss it belongs to. But even though they are all so different, they all have one goal in mind: to stop the player right in their tracks!... Or do they? GAUNTLET also takes a lot of inspiration from UNDERTALE, and because of that, it allows the player to choose how they want to approach the game. Are they going to show no mercy towards their enemies? Or are they going to spare these charismatic characters and try to achieve their goal by other means? Because of this, GAUNTLET is planned to have three main endings: a good, neutral, and bad ending. GAUNTLET will also feature all of my original pixel art and music, which are being hand-crafted to match the atmosphere and identity I want the game to have. As the game is still in early development, this page will showcase the beginning of my thought process, art journey, and the ideas that are shaping GAUNTLET into the game I want it to become. If you’re even more interested in the development process, you can also check out my Instagram page through the link in the footer or the contact page! I post weekly dev talks sharing the game’s progress, new ideas, and whatever I’ve been working on.

04 — DEVELOPMENT

Development Process

01

Concept and Initial Planning

GAUNTLET is still in its early stages of development, so I started by brainstorming the initial story and defining the main theme for each boss. From there, I began sketching character designs before moving on to the pixel art and the first gameplay prototypes.

02

Art Process

Since this is my first game where I am designing not only the UI, but also the characters and environments, creating the pixel art for GAUNTLET has been a real challenge. At the same time, it has been one of the most rewarding parts of the project and has helped me improve my art skills considerably.
