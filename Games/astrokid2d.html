01 — OVERVIEW

Game Description

AstroKid2D is a 2D Endless shooter that was part of my Unity Engine course at EBAC. It was my second project ever made and incorporates simple but extremely important aspects of development with the Unity Engine, such as prefab management,character movement, physics, animations, collectables, enemies, VFX, scriptable objects and SFX. The game was later updated at August 2026 to incorporate my new art(mainly UI), music and sound effects. .

05 — DEVELOPMENT

Development Process

01

First Steps and Core Mechanics

At first the focus was to setup the environment and the art (disponibilized by the school) as I was learning the basics of the Unity Engine, after that, I started working on the core mechanics of the game.

02

Side Mechanics

After implementing the core gameplay of AstroKid2D, the focus was to improve the player experience with elements such as collectible coins, more active enemies and score and better UI.

03

Polishing

After the core gameplay cycle was implemented, it was time to improve even more the player experience by adding vfx, sfx, music, add fresher animations and physics.

04

Testing and Finished Results

After everything was polished, I started testing multiple player actions and asked family and friends to playtest my game, as it was my first small-medium-sized project. After a lot of bug-fixing and more testing, I was very content with the state the game was in, and decided to call it finished.
